Where the designs come from

The short version

Here is something I love. That little floral print everyone calls "Provençal", the very French one, actually started as an Indian print. Indian cotton reached France in the 1600s, France banned it to protect its own mills, and then quietly copied it. My designs skip the French detour and go straight back to the Persian and Mughal work it all came from.

These are original designs, printed on silk. They are not block-printed or embroidered, and I do not borrow any craft's name to sell them. If it is the handmade, hand-blocked thing you want, please buy that from the artisans who make it. This is its own thing, and I would rather be honest about that.
